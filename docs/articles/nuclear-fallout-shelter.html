CBRN Defense: Nuclear Detonation & Ionizing Fallout Protocol

Following a tactical or strategic nuclear surface burst, particulate fallout descending downwind between 15 minutes and 2 hours represents the primary fatal hazard for non-blast survivors.

#### 1. Immediate Flash & Blast Reaction:

  • Never look at the fireball: Instantaneous retinal thermal burns and flash blindness occur at up to 50 miles.
  • Immediate Prone Dive: Drop flat behind reinforced masonry or earth depressions, facing away from the epicenter. Cover head and neck, tuck thumbs into ears, and keep mouth open to equalize atmospheric overpressure across eardrums.

#### 2. The Exponential 7/10 Rule of Radiation Decay:

Fallout fission products decay exponentially following the formula: $R_t = R_1 \times t^{-1.2}$. For every 7-fold increase in elapsed time, radiological exposure decreases by a factor of 10:

  • Hour 1 ($t = 1$ hr): Peak baseline exposure rate $R_1$ (e.g., lethal 100 R/hr).
  • Hour 7 ($t = 7$ hr): Radiation rate drops to $1/10$ of baseline (10 R/hr).
  • Hour 49 (~2 days, $7 \times 7$ hr): Radiation drops to $1/100$ of baseline (1 R/hr) — brief mission-critical exterior sorties permissible.
  • Hour 343 (~2 weeks, $7 \times 49$ hr): Radiation drops to $1/1,000$ of baseline (0.1 R/hr) — standard civil shelter egress threshold.

#### 3. Radiation Shielding & Halving-Thickness Values:

To cut gamma and neutron dose rates in half, establish barriers meeting these minimum thickness thresholds:

  • Lead ($Pb$): 1.0 cm (0.4 inches)
  • Structural Steel: 2.5 cm (1.0 inch)
  • Poured Solid Concrete: 6.0 cm (2.4 inches)
  • Packed Earth / Sand: 9.0 cm (3.6 inches)
  • Water: 18.0 cm (7.2 inches)
  • Doctrine: Subterranean basements with 2 feet of concrete/earth overhead provide a Protection Factor (PF) of 40 to 100+.

    • #### 4. FDA Potassium Iodide (iOSAT KI) Dosage Protocol:

    Administer only upon emergency broadcast alert of approaching radioiodine ($^{131}\text{I}$) fallout plume. Saturates the thyroid gland to prevent radioactive isotope absorption:

    • Adults (18+ years) & Pregnant/Lactating: 130 mg (1 full tablet) once daily.
    • Children (3 to 18 years, < 150 lbs): 65 mg (1/2 tablet) once daily.
    • Infants & Toddlers (1 month to 3 years): 32 mg (1/4 tablet) once daily.
    • Neonates (Birth to 1 month): 16 mg single dose dissolved in liquid.
    • Critical Medical Notice: KI protects only against thyroid cancer from ingested/inhaled $^{131}\text{I}$. It offers zero protection against external penetrating gamma or neutron radiation.
