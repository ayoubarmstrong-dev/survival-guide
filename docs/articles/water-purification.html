Emergency Hydration Standards

The human body can survive no longer than 3 days without potable water. The non-negotiable operational baseline is 3.5 to 4.0 liters per person per day (2 liters for direct metabolic hydration, 1.5–2 liters for food rehydration and basic hygiene).

#### 1. Multi-Stage Water Purification Sequence:

  1. Pre-Filtration: Strain raw water through tightly woven cotton or a bandanna to remove macro-sediment and debris.
  2. Bio-Sand & Activated Charcoal Filtration: Pass clarified water through layered fine sand (10 cm), coarse sand (5 cm), crushed hardwood charcoal (for chemical toxin and odor absorption), and gravel.
  3. Pathogen Elimination (Disinfection):
  4. Rolling Boil: Bring water to a vigorous rolling boil for 1 full minute (3 full minutes at altitudes above 2,000 meters / 6,500 feet).
  5. Chemical Chlorination (Plain 5–6% Unscented Household Bleach): Add 2 drops (0.1 mL) per liter of clear water (4 drops if cloudy). Seal and allow to stand undisturbed for 30 minutes. A faint chlorine scent must be detectable.
