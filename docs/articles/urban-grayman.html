Operating in Collapsed Municipal Environments

During prolonged civil collapse, drawing attention is fatal. Overt tactical equipment (camo fatigues, military plate carriers, tactical packs) signals resource possession and invites targeted ambush.

#### 1. Core Principles of Low-Signature Movement (The Gray Man):

  • Wear nondescript, weathered everyday apparel in neutral tones (gray, navy, worn denim, muted brown).
  • Conceal survival gear inside standard urban daypacks (school or commuter bags), avoiding MOLLE webbing systems.
  • Walk along building peripheries and shadowed margins; avoid crossing open intersections or public squares during daylight.

#### 2. Urban Resource Extraction:

  • Residential and commercial water heaters (hot water tanks) hold 40 to 120 gallons of potable water accessible via the bottom drain valve even after municipal pressure fails.
  • Commercial building roof tanks frequently hold filtered reserves; access via internal service ladders.
