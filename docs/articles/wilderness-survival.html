Wilderness Survival Priorities (The Rule of 3s)

  • 3 Minutes without oxygen.
  • 3 Hours without regulated thermal core shelter in harsh weather (hypothermia is the primary wilderness killer).
  • 3 Days without water.
  • 3 Weeks without food.

#### 1. Thermal Insulation & Debris Huts:

  • Never sleep directly on bare ground; conductive ground contact drains core body temperature rapidly.
  • Construct an insulating bedding layer of dry leaves, pine needles, and brush at least 30 cm (12 inches) thick before laying down.
  • Form a low-profile A-frame structure piled with 60 cm of dry forest debris to trap body heat naturally.

#### 2. Universal Edibility Testing for Unknown Vegetation:

  • Test one specific plant part (leaf, root, or stem) at a time.
  • Rub crushed plant matter against the sensitive inside skin of the wrist; wait 15 minutes for rash or burning.
  • Touch a trace amount to the outer lip for 15 minutes.
  • Place a small sample on the tongue without chewing or swallowing; spit out immediately if intense bitterness, burning, or soapy tastes occur.
