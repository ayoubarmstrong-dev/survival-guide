Long-Term Food Storage Strategy (90-Day Reserve)

When municipal power grids and national supply chains collapse, domestic refrigeration ceases within 24 to 48 hours. Strategic long-term preservation relies strictly on low-moisture, low-fat dry goods (moisture content below 10%).

#### 1. Core Staples with Extended Shelf Life (10 to 25+ Years):

  • Polished White Rice: Lasts up to 25-30 years when properly sealed in Mylar bags with oxygen absorbers. (Avoid brown rice for long-term storage due to natural oils that turn rancid within 6-12 months).
  • Dried Legumes (Lentils, Pinto Beans, Chickpeas): High protein and essential micronutrient density; maintains nutritional value for 20+ years.
  • Pure Raw Honey: Indefinite shelf life; serves as a high-density glucose source and topical antibacterial agent for minor lacerations.
  • Coarse Rock/Sea Salt: Non-perishable mineral essential for electrolyte balance and meat curing/preservation.
  • Rolled Oats: Excellent complex carbohydrates requiring minimal water and fuel to prepare.

    • #### 2. Professional Packaging Protocol (Mylar + Oxygen Scavengers):

    1. Utilize food-grade 5-gallon (20-liter) HDPE plastic pails.
    2. Insert a heavy-duty Mylar bag (5 to 7 mil thickness minimum).
    3. Fill with dry grains, leaving 2 inches at the top.
    4. Place a 2,000cc oxygen absorber (O2 scavenger) immediately prior to sealing.
    5. Seal the top edge using a household iron or heat sealer at 375°F (190°C), pressing out residual air.
    6. Seal the outer bucket lid with a rubber gasket hammer-lock.
