Long-Term Strategic Food Reserve (FEMA & WHO Nutrition Doctrine)

When modern electrical grids and municipal supply chains fail, strategic survival relies strictly on non-perishable, low-moisture (<10%) nutrient-dense staples.

#### 1. WHO & FEMA Daily Caloric Baselines:

  • Base Sedentary Survival: 2,100 kcal per person per day (sheltered adult baseline).
  • Heavy Manual Labor & Freezing Weather: 2,400 to 2,800 kcal per person per day (debris clearing, patrol, structural repairs).
  • Strategic Macronutrient Balance:
  • Carbohydrates (55%–60%): White rice, rolled oats, hard red wheat (stable glycogen maintenance).
  • Proteins (15%–20%): Dried pinto beans, lentils, chickpeas, freeze-dried meats.
  • Lipids / Fats (25%–30%): Coconut oil, canned ghee, peanut butter (dense 9 kcal/gram energy).

    • #### 2. Professional Packaging Protocol (Mylar + Oxygen Scavengers):

    1. Pail Setup: Use food-grade 5-gallon (20-liter) HDPE plastic containers.
    2. Heavy-Duty Barrier: Insert a 7-mil aluminum-laminated Mylar bag (moisture and oxygen transmission rate < 0.001 cc/m²/day).
    3. Fill Density: Fill dry grains to within 2 inches of the rim.
    4. Oxygen Scavenging: Drop a 2,000cc iron-based oxygen absorber into the top layer immediately before heat sealing. Absorbs up to 400 mL of residual gaseous oxygen, bringing chamber levels below 0.1% to eliminate insect larvae and lipid rancidity.
    5. Hermetic Heat Seal: Seal bag lip using a 375°F (190°C) iron, leaving a 1-inch vent to expel remaining air before final closure.
    6. Contingency Evacuation (72-Hour Grab Kits): Maintain ready-to-eat freeze-dried meal pouches (e.g. ReadyWise 72-Hour Kits) providing instant 1,800+ kcal/day without requiring cooking fuel.
