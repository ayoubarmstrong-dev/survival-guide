Decentralized Energy Production

In post-grid civilization, electrical generation is utilized for high-utility tasks: radio communications, ultraviolet water sterilizers, and perimeter security lighting.

#### 1. Continuous Anaerobic Biogas Digesters:

  • Inoculate a sealed 55-gallon drum with equal parts organic waste and water (slurry consistency).
  • Anaerobic bacteria break down cellulose, generating combustible methane (CH4) gas within 14 to 21 days at ambient temperatures between 25°C and 35°C.
  • Filter raw gas through steel wool (iron shavings) to scrub corrosive and toxic hydrogen sulfide (H2S) prior to cooking burner intake.

#### 2. Mechanical Hydro and Wind Dynamos:

  • Standard 12-volt automotive alternators can be driven by small stream waterwheels or geared bicycle pedals to maintain 12V deep-cycle battery banks continuously.
