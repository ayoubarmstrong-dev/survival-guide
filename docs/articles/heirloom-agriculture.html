Securing Multi-Year Post-Crisis Nutrition

Commercial agricultural seeds (F1 Hybrids) produce sterile or genetically unstable second-generation crops. Post-crisis survival hinges exclusively on Heirloom (Open-Pollinated, Non-GMO) seed varieties.

#### 1. Long-Term Seed Vault Maintenance:

  • Dry harvested seeds until they shatter rather than bend (moisture content below 8%).
  • Seal inside airtight glass mason jars with crushed charcoal packets to absorb ambient humidity; store subterraneanly at stable temperatures below 10°C (50°F).

#### 2. Biological Soil Regeneration:

  • Maintain hot compost piles: 2 parts carbon-rich brown material (straw, dry foliage) to 1 part nitrogen-rich green matter (crop trimmings, safe manure).
  • Implement nitrogen-fixing crop rotation (legumes, clover, field peas) alternating with caloric grains to preserve topsoil viability without industrial fertilizers.
